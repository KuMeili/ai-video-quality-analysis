# ai-video-quality-analysis
Measuring perceived quality of AI marketing video (lip-sync, timing, naturalness) vs engagement. Data analyst portfolio

##The Problem
AI video tools made marketing video look cheap but lip-sync and dubbing mismatch make content feel fake - and marketers have no way to measure it.

##The Question
Does the perceived quality of AI marketing video correlate with engagement?

## Scoring Rubric (Perceived Quality Score - PQS)
Each video is scored 1 - 5 on Four dimensions. The PQS is the average of the four.

### 1. Lip-Sync accuracy
1 - Clearly wrong. Mouth movements doesn't match the audio, viewer notices within the first 2 seconds without trying.

2 - Obviously off. Mouth and audio are roughly aligned but consistently out of sync. Noticeable even on a casual first watch.

3 - Slightly off. Mostly aligned, but there are moments where the mouth shape doesn't fit the word. You notice if you're paying attention, but it may not register on a quick scroll.

4 - Mostly right. Alignment is good. Only one or two brief moments look slightly unnatural.

5 - Natural.  Mouth and audio match naturally like human/humans speaking.

### 2. Audio - visual timing
1 - Words land noticeably off the mouth shape

2 - Mouth or movements of the actors and product are slightly notable lag

3 - Sound is to low or high to understand what is being said

4 - Audio is off just a bit either in the beginning or end of video

5 - Perfect alignment 

### 3. Naturalness
1 - Uncanny, robotic, distracting both product and actors

2 - Noticeable in first few seconds something is off 

3 - Every movement is stiff but passable

4 - Natural movements are slightly off on product and actors

5 - Everything flows naturally like a human

### 4. Viewer trust
1 - "This is fake/AI " instantly

2 - Product or Actors have unnatural movements

3 - "Something is off" guessing if it's not AI

4 - Passable as AI but has natural movements

5 - Fully trust of the marketing product and brand
